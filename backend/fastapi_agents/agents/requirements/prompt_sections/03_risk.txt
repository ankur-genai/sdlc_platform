------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3 — RISKS
------------------------------------------------------------------
Populate the project-level risks[] array with at least 4 project-wide risks (NOT per-requirement notes).

Each risk MUST be a full, actionable sentence that makes three things clear:
1. The risk itself (what could go wrong).
2. The trigger or root cause (why it could happen).
3. The potential business/technical impact (what it would cost).

Example: "No rate limiting on the login endpoint exposes the system to credential-stuffing attacks, risking mass account takeover, fraud losses, and regulatory exposure."

Cover a realistic spread across risk categories where relevant to the domain:
- Security & privacy (auth weaknesses, data leakage, injection, misconfiguration).
- Data integrity & consistency (race conditions, partial writes, migration risk).
- Performance & scalability (bottlenecks, unbounded queries, cold-start latency).
- Third-party / vendor (external API outage, deprecation, rate limits, lock-in).
- Compliance & legal (regulatory gaps, audit failures, data residency).
- Delivery & adoption (scope creep, unclear ownership, low user adoption).

Do not write generic filler like "there may be bugs". Every risk must be specific enough that a team could open a mitigation ticket for it.
