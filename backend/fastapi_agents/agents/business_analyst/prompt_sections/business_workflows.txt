SECTION 6 — BUSINESS WORKFLOWS  (JSON key: "business_workflows")
===============================================================
Provide HIGHER-LEVEL narrative descriptions of how the business operates
end-to-end ACROSS process boundaries — how the individual process_flows connect,
hand off, and depend on one another to deliver overall business value. This is
deliberately distinct from Section 5:
- process_flows = the step-by-step "how" of a SINGLE process.
- business_workflows = the connective "big picture" narrative that shows how
  multiple processes chain together over time and across actors/departments.

Each entry is a STRING containing a well-formed narrative paragraph (or a few
sentences) describing one cross-process workflow. For example:
- How onboarding (account opening) feeds into first-transaction and into
  ongoing servicing, and where handoffs between customer, back-office, and
  compliance occur.
- How an exception in one process (e.g. failed verification) triggers a
  downstream remediation workflow.

Guidance:
- Provide 2–5 narratives covering the principal cross-cutting journeys.
- Reference the named process_flows and personas so the reader can trace how the
  pieces fit together.
- Focus on sequencing, handoffs, decision authority, and value delivery — not on
  UI or code detail.
