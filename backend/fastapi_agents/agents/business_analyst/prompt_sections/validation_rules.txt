SECTION 7 — VALIDATION RULES  (JSON key: "validation_rules")
============================================================
Capture CROSS-CUTTING data and business validation rules that apply across
multiple stories/processes — the rules that keep data correct, consistent, and
trustworthy regardless of which feature is exercised. These are business rules,
not code; state them so QA can write tests and developers can enforce them.

Each entry is a STRING expressing one specific, testable rule. Good rules are
concrete and unambiguous. Examples of the expected style:
- "All monetary amounts must be validated and re-checked server-side even when
  validated client-side; client validation is never authoritative."
- "A customer cannot have more than 5 active disputes open simultaneously."
- "Email addresses must be unique per customer account and validated against
  RFC 5322 before persistence."
- "Any state-changing financial operation must be idempotent for the same
  client-supplied idempotency key within a 24-hour window."
- "Dates of birth must place the customer at 18+ years old at account creation."

Guidance:
- Cover data-integrity rules, cross-entity constraints, authorisation/ownership
  rules, and domain-specific regulatory validations implied by the requirements.
- Provide a meaningful set (typically 6–15) — enough to protect the core data
  and business invariants, but every rule must be real and grounded, not filler.
- Prefer server-authoritative, security-conscious phrasing.
