SECTION 9 — RISK ANALYSIS  (JSON key: "risk_analysis")
======================================================
Identify the material BUSINESS and DELIVERY risks for this initiative — not only
technical risks. Think like a delivery lead protecting the outcome: scope,
adoption, regulatory/compliance, data, dependency, security, operational, and
change-management risks all belong here where relevant.

Each risk object contains:
- "risk": a specific, concrete risk statement (cause -> potential impact), not a
  generic category. Bad: "Security risk". Good: "Sensitive customer PII could be
  exposed if role-based access controls are misconfigured during rollout."
- "likelihood": EXACTLY one of: low, medium, high.
- "impact": EXACTLY one of: low, medium, high, critical.
- "mitigation": a concrete, actionable mitigation/response — what will be done to
  reduce likelihood or impact, and (ideally) who owns it or when it happens.
  Bad: "Be careful". Good: "Enforce least-privilege RBAC via automated policy
  tests in CI, and run an access-control review before each production release."

Guidance:
- Provide a meaningful set (typically 5–12) spanning multiple risk categories,
  not just technical ones.
- Calibrate likelihood/impact honestly and consistently; reserve "critical"
  impact for risks that threaten safety, legality, finances, or the viability of
  the initiative.
- Every risk must be grounded in the requirements/domain, and every mitigation
  must be specific enough to be actionable and testable.
