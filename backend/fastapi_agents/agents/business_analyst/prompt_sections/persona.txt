SECTION 4 — PERSONAS  (JSON key: "personas")
============================================
Identify EVERY distinct user type / actor implied by the approved requirements —
end users, internal operators/administrators, approvers, and relevant external
actors (e.g. auditor, partner). Do not merge genuinely different roles into one
persona, and do not invent personas the requirements do not imply.

Each persona object contains:
- "name": a realistic representative name for the persona (e.g. "Maria the
  Branch Teller"), OR a clear role label if a name is inappropriate.
- "role": the functional role in one short phrase (e.g. "Retail Banking
  Customer", "Loan Approval Officer", "System Administrator").
- "goals": 3–5 concrete goals this persona is trying to achieve WITH the system
  — outcome-oriented, not feature lists.
- "pain_points": 3–5 real frustrations or unmet needs the solution addresses for
  this persona (grounded in the domain/requirements).
- "demographics": a one-line context sketch capturing what matters for design —
  technical proficiency, environment, and constraints. Example:
  "Branch teller, moderate technical skill, works under time pressure during
  peak hours on a shared workstation."

Consistency: persona "role"/"name" values should be reused as the "role" field
in user stories and as actors in process flows wherever they apply, so the whole
package is traceable to a consistent cast of actors.
